# 07 — UX and Visual System

## Desired feeling

Premium editorial beauty product + trustworthy decision tool.

Not:
- clinical dashboard
- “AI cyber” interface
- generic SaaS
- cluttered cards
- health alarm UI

---

# Visual direction

## Palette

Use restrained neutrals:
- warm off-white
- ink / near-black
- one muted accent such as plum, oxblood, or deep berry
- subtle confidence/status colors only where needed

Do not use traffic-light green/red for “skin quality.”

## Typography

Use a sophisticated display face or strong serif for marketing headlines and a clean sans-serif for UI.

Avoid overly playful beauty fonts.

## Layout

Mobile-first.

Use large spacing.

Decision screens should have:
- one main conclusion
- 2–4 reason chips
- one next action

Evidence is progressive disclosure.

---

# Screen-by-screen

## Landing

Headline:
**Your skin gets a vote before makeup reaches your cart.**

Subhead:
Compare the products you are already considering using your current skin signals, your own sensitivities, and source-backed product facts.

CTA:
**Compare my products**

Secondary:
**Run 60-second Judge Mode**

Small integrity line:
Decision support, not medical diagnosis.

## Moment screen

Prompt:
“What are you getting ready for?”

Keep lightweight.

## Selfie capture

Show 3 capture tips, not 10.

Provide sample mode.

Show consent before provider processing.

## Skin snapshot

Do not render 15 concerns.

Render only relevant signals:
- redness
- texture
- hydration
- oiliness
- acne signal if provided

Use neutral language:
“Current signals”

Not:
“Your skin is bad.”

## Product compare

Three elegant product columns/cards.

Show:
- finish
- coverage
- key user constraint status
- evidence coverage

Avoid ingredient walls.

## Decision

Large:
**Try Candidate B first**

Reason chips:
- matches medium coverage
- fragrance constraint satisfied
- stronger evidence
- natural finish preference

Secondary:
“Why not Candidate A?”

Primary:
**See it on me**

## VTO

Full-bleed or high-impact before/after.

Do not cover the visual with analytics.

Below:
“Preview generated with YouCam Makeup VTO.”

## Decision Receipt

Human summary first.

Expandable:
- inputs
- rules
- evidence
- provider traces
- receipt hash

---

# Motion

Use motion for:
- ranking reorder
- evidence reveal
- VTO before/after
- Judge Mode timeline

Support reduced motion.

Never let animation delay the judge.

---

# Judge Mode

Use a thin progress rail:
1. Skin
2. Compare
3. Decide
4. Try
5. Prove

Auto-advance only where safe.
Allow manual “Next”.

Never hide the Replay badge.

---

# Copy style

Short, calm, specific.

Good:
“Fragrance status is unknown, so we lowered confidence.”

Bad:
“Our advanced AI detected this product may trigger acne.”

Good:
“You told us to avoid fragrance.”

Bad:
“This ingredient is dangerous for your skin.”
